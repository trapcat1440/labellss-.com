There is a range of tasks most designers are involved with on a regular basis. A designer may not be expected to do every one of the activities , but their supervision is essential. A designer is ultimately responsible for the design of each collection as it moves from inspiration to production.Even if designers have a support staff of design assistant , fabric researchers , and developers , and patternmakers, computer design specialist and technical designers , they must supervise and direct their design room team in an appropriate and timely manner. Principal tasks include performing research , following trends , creating mood / concept boards , source fabrics , and gain knowledge of fabrics. Perform research to establish mood and accumulate visual references for collections. Follow trends include keeping up to date on design and market trends directly and indirectly related to the field.Create mood / concept boards includes editing research to show a direction and present to retailers and editors for feedback

Fashion measures people's attitudes , morals , views , and alienations in society , reflects economic status and creates tribes that one aspires to join. It nonverbally communicates information about a person and their affiliations. Fall and spring are the primary seasons with the largest collections, whereas the others are smaller “capsule” collections.High priced collections have fewer deliveries because the clothing is more labor intensive and expensive to produce.Lower-priced collections such as budget may have new deliveries every week to entice customers. Many stores will keep multiple deliveries on the floor , as such designers must be sensitive to how fabrics and colors flow from one delivery to the next. Fall , in stores by september,includes traditional clothing such as gray , black , and taupe along with any trending colors. Fabrics must be textural and heavy to suit the weather.Holiday , in stores by November , includes a smaller collection with colors such as blacks , metallics , champagne , and jewel tones. Fabrics are usually dressy and aimed such as velvet , lace and satins. Resort , in stores by December/January , include a small capsule collection , with colors such as pastel and colors that are bright. Swimsuits and spring clothing are usually introduced . Spring , in stores by February , includes a larger collection with fabrics such as cottons, silk knits , and generally lighter colors.Summer , in stores by april/may , includes a collection with silhouettes more casual and less layered.
