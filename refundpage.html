Refund Policy

Last Updated: July 19, 2026

If your purchase does not meet your expectations, we offer a straightforward return and refund process.

Return Period
You may request a return within 30 days of receiving your order. Items must be unworn, unused, and returned in their original condition.

Return Eligibility

To be eligible for a return, your item must meet the following requirements:

  • Item must be unused and unworn
  • Item must include original tags and packaging
  • Item must not show signs of washing, damage, or alteration
  • Proof of purchase must be provided

Items Not Eligible For Return

  • Items that have been worn or damaged after delivery
  • Items that do not fit

How To Request A Return

To begin a return request, contact our support team with:

  • Your order number
  • Email used during checkout
  • Description of the issue
  • Location of purchase

Once your request is approved, you will receive return instructions.

Refund Process

After we receive and inspect your returned item, we will notify you regarding your refund status.

Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method. Processing times are instant once agency approves of return. Our refunds are issued through third party vendors or post office of government agencies.

Damaged Or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or you receive the wrong product, please contact us as soon as possible. We will review the issue and work toward a resolution.

Return Shipping

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item received was incorrect, defective, or damaged upon arrival.

Order Cancellations

Orders may only be cancelled before they enter the processing or fulfillment stage. Once an order has shipped, our standard return process will apply.
